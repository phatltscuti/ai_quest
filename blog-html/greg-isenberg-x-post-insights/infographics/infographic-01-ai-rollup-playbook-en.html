AI Roll-up Playbook: buy a services firm, let agents run the back office

Keep the clients & invoices, rebuild how work is delivered → EBITDA from ~5–10% toward a 30–40% target

1

Sourcing

  • License boards, state filings, industry directories
  • Owners in their 60s–70s with no successor
  • Reach the owner before a broker lists it
2

Scorecard

  • 7 criteria: repeatable work 20%, checkable output 20%, recurring revenue 15%…
  • < 60% = a job, not an acquisition
  • ≥ 80% = deserves an LOI
3

Diligence with work samples

  • Ask for 20–50 real, anonymized jobs
  • Build the automation map: volume × hours
  • Classify: automate / automate+review / assist / keep human
4

The math (illustrative)

  • Revenue stays at $2.0M
  • Margin 10% → 30%: EBITDA $200k → $600k
  • Value at 4x: $800k → $2.4M
5

The rulebook is the asset

  • Interview senior staff → numbered rules with examples
  • Agent drafts → human corrects → corrections-log.md
  • Repeated fix → new rule + test case
6

First 100 days

  • Days 1–30: shadow mode, nothing visible to clients
  • Days 31–60: move the back office, every draft reviewed
  • Days 61–100: expand carefully

Guardrails

  • Reviewer can block, never ship
  • Preparer ships nothing on its own
  • Humans approve rules & send sensitive comms

Dashboard: 5 numbers every Monday

  • EBITDA margin · human minutes per job
  • Correction rate (should fall monthly)
  • Client retention · key-person retention

What breaks

  • Buying faster than you can integrate
  • Key people / clients leave
  • Automating trust away · over-powered agents · trusting headline numbers
