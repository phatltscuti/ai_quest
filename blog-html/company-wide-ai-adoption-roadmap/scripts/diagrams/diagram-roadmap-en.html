Company-wide AI adoption roadmap (synthesized from both decks)

Not “hand tools to everyone” but: define the destination → go deep in one department → link to business outcomes → build guardrails & a golden path → scale with an operating model
0

Aspire & Assess

Month 0–1
SmartHR
  • Define the target state as business outcomes, not user counts
  • Inventory scattered tools, costs and initiatives
  • Interview people inside and outside
✔ Exit gate: Mid-term goal + list of real problems
1

Narrow & deep pilot

Month 1–3
LMI
  • Pick one department as the “first customer”
  • Department head commits to a target (return + request)
  • AX promoter coaches ~2 months (66 days to form a habit)
✔ Exit gate: Members use AI daily in real work
2

Link to outcomes

Month 2–4
LMISmartHR
  • Draw an outcome map: AI use → leading → lagging → impact
  • Decide what the saved time is spent on
  • Measure 5 layers: cost → usage → initiatives → org → business
✔ Exit gate: Department head reviews the numbers regularly
3

Guardrails & Golden path

Month 3–5
LMISmartHR
  • Tier tool access: no-code → low-code → pro-code
  • Cross-functional risk team takes reports, updates rules
  • Standard path for internal tools: CPF → PSF → PMF → GTM
✔ Exit gate: Self-built tools pass security & cost review
4

Scale & operate

Month 5–6+
SmartHRLMI
  • Executives own it; AI Ops is an enabler, not a gatekeeper
  • Governance WG + Promotion WG + AI Lead per unit
  • Monthly report to management, knowledge sharing
✔ Exit gate: Each new unit re-passes the same PMF criteria
Sources: Link and Motivation (PEK2026, 2026-09-25) & SmartHR (2026-09-28) on Speaker Deck — synthesized and interpreted by the author. Month ranges are the author's proposal, not from the slides.
