# DEMO A — PROMPT FULL PRESENTATION (dùng khi tenant đã có tính năng 06/2026)

Create a fully editable 6-slide presentation for an internal executive review titled
"Northwind Customer Portal MVP".

Audience: delivery director, presales lead, operations lead, and solution architect.
Goal: approve a 10-week MVP discovery and delivery plan.

Required presentation format:
1. Title — project name, "Executive approval brief", July 2026.
2. Executive summary — customer problem, proposed MVP, and the decision requested.
3. Current-state problem — 3 concise pain points and one prominent metric.
4. Proposed solution — two columns: MVP capabilities on the left and a simple system flow on the right.
5. Scope and 10-week delivery plan — In scope / Out of scope plus a horizontal timeline.
6. Success metrics, risks, and next steps — three KPI cards, two key risks, and three numbered actions.

Content rules:
- Ground all facts and metrics in "Project brief — Northwind".
- Do not invent financial figures, customer quotes, or commitments.
- Add source citations where the interface supports them.
- Use concise executive language: no more than 45 words per slide, excluding labels.

Visual rules:
- Match "STYLE_Reference_Deck".
- 16:9, clean executive layout, white background, navy and cyan accents.
- Use native editable text boxes, shapes, diagrams, tables, and charts.
- Keep a small footer: "Confidential — internal draft".
- Avoid decorative stock imagery unless it improves understanding.

# ANSWERS FOR GEMINI FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS

Tone: concise, executive, evidence-based.
Audience familiarity: they know the customer but have not reviewed this proposal.
Primary decision: approve the 10-week MVP plan and week-1 operations workshop.
Priority: preserve factual accuracy and editability over visual decoration.

# OUTLINE EDITS TO MAKE BEFORE APPROVE

- Ensure there are exactly 6 slides.
- Keep slide 2 focused on the decision, not project history.
- Make slide 4 show: Customer → Portal → TMS REST API (read-only).
- Make slide 5 separate In scope and Out of scope.
- Slide 6 must include both risks: API rate limits and legal review.
