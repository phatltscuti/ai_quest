FULL PRESENTATION PROMPT — KHÔNG CẦN FILE NGUỒN
================================================

Create a fully editable 6-slide presentation for an internal executive review titled
"Northwind Customer Portal MVP".

Audience:
- Delivery director
- Presales lead
- Operations lead
- Solution architect

Goal:
Obtain approval for a 10-week MVP plan and the week-1 operations workshop.

Use only the following project facts:
- The support team spends more than 12 hours per week copying shipment status
  from a legacy TMS into email replies.
- Customers do not have a self-service shipment-status dashboard.
- Customer NPS dropped 8 points in Q1.
- The MVP includes secure login, shipment list and filters, shipment details
  with a status timeline, PDF label download, admin CSV export, and read-only
  integration with the existing TMS REST API.
- The 10-week MVP includes authentication, shipment list and filters,
  shipment details and timeline, PDF label download, admin CSV export,
  and read-only TMS integration.
- Billing, returns, and multi-tenant white labeling are out of scope.
- Success targets are a 40% reduction in "Where is my order?" tickets within
  60 days and weekly active users equal to at least 25% of active B2B accounts.
- Risk 1: TMS API rate limits may require caching.
- Risk 2: Legal review is required for data shown to customer sub-accounts.
- Next steps: operations workshop in week 1, TMS API technical spike in week 2,
  and wireframe sign-off in week 3.

Required presentation format:
1. Title
   - Project name
   - "Executive approval brief"
   - July 2026

2. Executive summary
   - Customer problem
   - Proposed MVP
   - Decision requested
   - Use three concise blocks

3. Current-state problem
   - Three concise pain points
   - One prominent metric

4. Proposed solution
   - Two-column layout
   - Left: MVP capabilities
   - Right: native editable flow:
     Customer → Portal → TMS REST API (read-only)

5. Scope and 10-week delivery plan
   - Clearly separate In scope and Out of scope
   - Add a native editable horizontal timeline

6. Success metrics, risks, and next steps
   - Three KPI cards
   - Both stated risks
   - Three numbered next actions

Content rules:
- Use only the project facts provided in this prompt.
- Do not invent financial figures, customer quotes, revenue, cost savings,
  deadlines, or commitments.
- Use concise executive language.
- Use no more than 45 words per slide, excluding labels and the footer.

Visual rules:
- Use 16:9.
- Use a clean professional design with a white background and navy/cyan accents.
- Use native editable text boxes, shapes, diagrams, tables, and charts.
- Keep strong visual hierarchy and generous whitespace.
- Add the footer "Confidential — internal draft".
- Avoid decorative stock imagery unless it improves understanding.
