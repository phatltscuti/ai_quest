MEETING NOTES — Client requirements interview (reference deal)

Client: Acme Retail Co., Ltd.
Project: Customer loyalty mini-portal (MVP)
Date: 2026-03-18
Duration: 30 minutes
Attendees: PM (host), Solution architect, Client IT lead

────────────────────────────────────────
Purpose
────────────────────────────────────────
Clarify loyalty MVP scope and POS integration constraints before formal proposal.

────────────────────────────────────────
Key discussion points
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Scope: Client wants earn/burn only in MVP; marketing campaigns deferred to phase 2.
2. POS: AcmePOS v12 on-prem; staging URL promised within 1 week.
3. Auth: Client prefers OAuth2 client credentials for integration service account (not user-delegated).
4. Operations: Support window 08:00–20:00 ICT; no 24/7 SLA for MVP.

────────────────────────────────────────
Decisions
────────────────────────────────────────
- POS catalog remains source of truth for SKU master data.
- No native mobile app in MVP.

────────────────────────────────────────
Open questions (carried to proposal)
────────────────────────────────────────
- Final staging URL and firewall whitelist timeline.
- Campaign module budget owner (marketing vs IT).

────────────────────────────────────────
Lessons learned (for future pitches)
────────────────────────────────────────
- Always confirm integration credential model in first call.
- Separate MVP scope from roadmap items in writing to avoid scope creep.
- Include explicit support window in commercial section.

────────────────────────────────────────
Action items
────────────────────────────────────────
- PM: Send draft proposal within 3 business days.
- Architect: Document REST endpoints required from loyalty engine.
