## 2. Safety notes

- Do not use the purifier in bathrooms or where relative humidity exceeds 80%.
- Keep at least 30 cm clearance on all sides and 1 m from heaters.
- Unplug before opening any cover. The UV-C lamp (Pro) switches off automatically when the front cover opens.

## 3. Setup (first use)

1. Unbox the purifier and remove ALL plastic packaging from the main filter (the filter ships sealed in a plastic bag inside the unit).
2. Place the purifier on a flat, hard floor at least 30 cm away from walls, curtains and furniture. Do not place it on carpet with long pile.
3. Plug the power cable into a 100-240 V outlet. Avoid shared multi-plug extension cords.
4. Press and hold the Power button for 3 seconds. The ring light turns white and the unit starts in Auto mode.
5. Let the sensor calibrate for the first 10 minutes. During calibration the air-quality ring blinks blue; do not move the unit.
6. (AQ-200 and AQ-200 Pro only) Install the AeroPure Home app, then press and hold Wi-Fi + Timer for 5 seconds until the Wi-Fi icon blinks, and follow the in-app pairing. Only 2.4 GHz networks are supported.
7. Choose a mode: Auto (recommended), Sleep (lowest noise, display off), or manual fan speed 1-4 (Turbo = speed 4).

## 6. Frequently asked questions

Q: Can I wash the main HEPA filter?
A: No. Only the pre-filter (PF-200) is washable. Washing the HEPA/carbon filter destroys it.

Q: Can I use the purifier in a bathroom?
A: No. Do not use the purifier where relative humidity is above 80% or where it can be splashed with water.

Q: Why is the air-quality ring red right after I turn it on?
A: During the first 10 minutes the sensor is calibrating. If the ring stays red for more than 1 hour in a closed room, clean the PM2.5 sensor lens.

Q: Does the purifier remove viruses?
A: The H13 HEPA filter captures 99.95% of particles at 0.3 microns in lab tests. AeroPure makes no medical claims.

Q: Does the AQ-200 support 5 GHz Wi-Fi?
A: No. Only 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi is supported on AQ-200 and AQ-200 Pro. The Lite has no Wi-Fi.

Q: How do I turn on child lock?
A: Hold the Lock (padlock) button for 3 seconds. Hold again for 3 seconds to unlock.

Q: How long does a filter last?
A: About 6 months (2,160 h) in normal use, or about 4 months with pets, smoking or heavy cooking. The filter indicator turns red at end of life.

Q: Is the filter the same for all models?
A: No. AQ-200 Lite and AQ-200 use FL-200H. AQ-200 Pro uses the larger FL-200P.

Q: What does the warranty cover?
A: Manufacturing defects for 2 years (Lite, AQ-200) or 3 years (Pro). Filters, pre-filters and UV-C lamps are consumables and are not covered.
