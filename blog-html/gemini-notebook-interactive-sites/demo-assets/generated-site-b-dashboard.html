March 2026: Wi-Fi Firmware 2.3 Spike

In range

Firmware 2.3 caused Wi-Fi reconnect failures (error code E11) on AQ-200 and AQ-200 Pro (AQ-200 Lite has no Wi-Fi). Wi-Fi/App tickets more than doubled from 22 in February to 47 in March (+113.6%), pushing average resolution time to a 10-month high of 31.2 h and pulling CSAT down to 3.9. Firmware 2.3.1 resolved the issue on 28 March, restoring Wi-Fi/App tickets to 30 and CSAT to 4.3 in April.

August 2026: Humidity & Sensor Spike

In range

Following the June–July summer haze sales peak (2,130 and 2,320 units), high August humidity triggered a surge in E06 (“sensor dirty”) tickets. Sensor tickets climbed from 21 in July to 35 in August (+66.7%), lifting total monthly tickets to a peak of 152, slowing resolution to 26.8 h, and dipping CSAT to 4.0. Adding an in-app sensor-cleaning reminder in September cut Sensor tickets back to 19 and lifted CSAT to 4.4.
