Placement & safety notes

  • Do not use in bathrooms or where relative humidity exceeds 80%.
  • Keep at least 30 cm clearance on all sides and 1 m from heaters; place on a flat, hard floor (not long-pile carpet).
  • Unplug before opening any cover. On the Pro, the UV-C lamp switches off automatically when the front cover opens.
  • Remove all plastic packaging from the main filter before first use, and allow 10 minutes for initial sensor calibration (ring blinks blue).
