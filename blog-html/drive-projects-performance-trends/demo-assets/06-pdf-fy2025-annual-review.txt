ANNUAL OPERATIONS REVIEW — FY 2025 (PDF source text)
Prepared by: PMO / Operations Analytics

────────────────────────────────────────
1. Year in review
────────────────────────────────────────
FY2025 ended with recovery in Q4 after Q3 migration impact. Full-year uptime averaged 99.48% (target 99.5%). Customer NPS finished at year-high 46.

────────────────────────────────────────
2. Quarter-over-quarter narrative
────────────────────────────────────────
Q1: Solid baseline; uptime slightly below target.
Q2: Best operational quarter — lowest P1 count, highest sprint completion.
Q3: Migration caused uptime dip and NPS drop; spend spike.
Q4: Stabilization — uptime and delivery metrics rebounded; cost optimization reduced run-rate.

────────────────────────────────────────
3. Strategic recommendations for 2026
────────────────────────────────────────
1) Schedule major infra changes outside peak support windows.
2) Automate quarterly metric roll-up into a single dashboard (reduce manual sheet consolidation).
3) Tie incident comms playbook to NPS recovery targets within 30 days of P1 events.
