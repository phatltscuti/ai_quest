# Storyboard – Scuti AI motion graphics intro (19s, 1920x1080, 30fps)

Brief: 19-second brand intro for Scuti AI (Vietnam-based system development company specializing in generative AI).
Audience: prospective clients (JP/global) and new team members. Action at the end: remember the brand + visit scuti.asia.
Facts used ONLY from the public site https://scuti.asia (checked 2026-10-03): tagline, 5 services, "2015 founded",
"400+ members (group)", "300+ projects", "6 offices (group)". No testimonials, ratings, prices or results invented.

Carrier object (one object carries the story across shots): the 5-node red "network" from the Scuti Ai logo.

| # | Time | On screen | Main movement | Secondary movement (overlapping) | Exit / transition to next shot |
|---|------|-----------|---------------|----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| 1 | 0.0–3.6 | Dark stage, one red node lights up, lines draw out into the 5-node network. Kicker "VIETNAM · GENERATIVE AI". Headline "We make services / *people love*" | Network builds node by node (draw lines) then slides left | Particles drift, grid scrolls, kicker letter-spacing tightens, headline words rise from mask | Headline leaves fast upward (0.35s); network flies TOWARD the camera (scale 1 -> 14) = foreground becomes the transition |
| 2 | 3.9–6.6 | Big line "by the power of **Gen AI.**" | Words punch in with overshoot, "Gen AI" in red with underline sweep | Network returns from behind at small scale, rotating | Text squeezes out, network moves to center = matched position for shot 3 |
| 3 | 6.6–11.2 | "What we build" + 5 service cards around the network: Gen AI Consulting, Generative AI OCR, Secure GAI (RAG), Dify Support, Software Outsourcing | Cards fly out from the network center along connector lines (back.out) | Network slowly rotates, connector lines draw, card glow | Hold ~2.5s to read, then cards collapse back into the center fast (power3.in) |
| 4 | 11.2–14.4 | 4 stat columns: Since 2015 / 400+ members (group) / 300+ projects / 6 offices (group) | Numbers count up, bars grow | Big faint network rotates behind, label fade-ins | Stats drop out, network shrinks to a point which becomes the dragon trail start |
| 5 | 14.4–19.0 | Stylised red dragon (illustrative mascot, built in SVG) swoops in, then logo lockup: network icon + "Scuti Ai" + tagline + scuti.asia | Dragon flight along a curve, then lockup builds | Dragon body trail draws, red "i" dots drop with bounce, slow push-in | Hold logo ~2s, fade to black in the last 0.4s |

Speed rule: hold just long enough to read -> leave fast -> slow down as the next piece arrives (expo/back eases, no linear moves except drift).
No empty frames: particles + grid always drift.

## Final timing after 2 critic rounds (v3, 18.0s)
Shot 1: 0-3.9s | Shot 2: 3.9-6.05s | Shot 3: 6.05-9.75s | Shot 4: 9.75-12.55s | Shot 5: 12.35-18.0s (dragon starts 0.2s before the shot, logo hold ~2.5s, fade last 0.4s).
